Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

You can book an appointment directly through our online booking system on this website, call our patient desk at +91 (011) 4982-7000, or send an email inquiry to appointments@meridianclinic.in. Same-day appointments are available for urgent cases.

We maintain an in-house automated pathology laboratory, 4D ultrasound scanner, digital X-ray, 12-lead ECG, Holter monitoring, and allergy testing facilities. Most lab reports are delivered within 4 to 6 hours into your Patient Portal.

Yes, we partner with major health insurance providers and TPAs in India for cashless reimbursement assistance. Our patient desk will assist you with pre-authorization documentation for day-care surgical procedures and specialized diagnostic panels.

Meridian Clinic strictly adheres to international clinical sterilization protocols. All procedure suites use HEPA air filtration system, class-B autoclave sterilization, and touchless sanitization stations throughout the facility.
